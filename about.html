Our story

Route to Discovery

We know that moment when you're watching a movie and wonder, "Where is this?" CinePath was born to turn that curiosity into an adventure.

Our goal is to map iconic film and TV locations across Turkey, enabling fans to visit these places, take photos, and relive those magical moments.

We are not just a platform; we are a community created by cinema enthusiasts, a living and constantly growing community.
